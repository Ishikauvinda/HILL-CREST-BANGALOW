Discover HILL CREST BANGALOW

HILL CREST BANGALOW is a tribute to the classic colonial architecture that echoes through this part of the island. With an elegant exterior fringed by carved fretwork, triangular tiled roofs, shuttered windows and stuccoed white columns it gives guests a glance in to the grandeur of the past. The interior boasts of a modern contemporary take on an old school Singhalese house of distinction or “Wallawa”.
